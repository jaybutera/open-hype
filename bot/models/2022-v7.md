# Model: displacement at a level, entry on the retrace (v7)

v6 with the entry at the near edge of the 5m gap. In four v7 sessions
(2026-10-15, 10-20, 10-22, 10-26) three orders were placed at the near edge
of the first 1m gap and none filled; on 10-26 the retrace stopped 3.4
points above the 1m edge, at the 5m gap's near edge, and went to the target
for 2.13R. The 5m gap is the same zone seen coarser and its near edge is
the shallowest retrace that counts; the 1m gap is inside it and is only
reached by a deeper retrace. v6 was written after 10-22, where the v6
profile On 2026-10-22 the v6 profile sold the last displacement
of a 50-point leg at 7656.0, six points above a vol strike that had held
7650 all session with the biggest builds, with the stop at the leg's origin
15 points away; price pinned on the strike, retraced to the put wall and
took the stop, then fell back to the strike. The baseline sold the same
retrace at 7676.5 from a limit that had rested two hours in the first
break's gap, stop above the put wall, target the strike, and took 2R.

The only setup you may trade.

## Definitions

- **Significant level**: a price from the level list in the journal's step 1.
  Nothing else qualifies.
- **Displacement**: one 5m bar, or two in a row, moving away from the level on
  the trade side with bodies that dwarf the bars around them, closing beyond
  the bar that reached the level, and leaving a gap. If you have to measure
  whether a bar is a displacement, it is not one. A level given up by a drift
  of small overlapping bars is a break without displacement; the retest of it
  from the other side is where the displacement may come.
- **Two forms**, by where price came from:
  - *Sweep*: price came from the trade side, wicked through the level, and the
    displacement took it back. The sweep wick is the structural extreme.
  - *Break*: price came from the other side and the displacement closed
    through the level. The structural extreme is the origin of the 1m leg
    that made the gap: the last 1m swing before the 1m bar that opened the
    gap, not the high or low of the whole 5m approach.
  Negative gamma below zero gamma produces breaks; positive gamma above zero
  gamma produces sweeps. Both are the model.
- **Fair value gap (FVG)**: the gap the displacement left. Read it on 1m
  inside the displacement leg first; the 5m gap (between the wicks of the
  bars either side of the displacement bar) is the same zone seen coarser.
  The entry zone is the 5m gap when the displacement leaves one; the 1m gap
  inside the leg only when the 5m bars overlap and leave none. The near
  edge is the edge price reaches first on the retrace, and the entry sits
  on it. When a leg has several displacement bars, the entry zone is the
  gap of the first one through the level, the one furthest from the leg's
  end: it is the one the retrace reaches with the stop still close and the
  wall still far.
- **Walls**: the OI put wall, the OI call wall, zero gamma, and a vol major
  that has held its strike over two or more reads with the biggest builds.
  A vol major that moved to the nearest strike since the last read is a
  level and a target, not a wall.
- **Stop**: beyond the structural extreme, a point or two past it, and
  beyond any wall within a few points of it (a stop under the put wall gets
  run by the retrace that tests the wall). Never inside the gap. A price
  with a reason, not a round number of points.
- **Target**: 2R, unless a wall, a vol strike, or a daily or 4h level sits
  between 1.5R and 3R on the trade side; then that price is the target (the
  strike price is touching, not the next one). In the break regime the wall
  is where the leg pins, so a target beyond it is not reached.

## Checklist

Fill every line in the journal before an order. A blank line means no trade.

1. Level: its price and its name from the level list.
2. Side: the regime that set it and the two readings that agree.
3. Displacement: the bar or bars by ET time, their bodies against the bars
   around them, and the extreme they closed through.
4. Gap: both edges, on 5m and on 1m, and which one is the entry zone.
5. Structural extreme, and the stop price beyond it.
6. Entry: a resting limit at the near edge of the entry zone, with the stop
   and target attached, placed once the bar after the displacement has
   closed. The paper engine fills a limit when a 1m bar closes through it, so
   a touch does not fill; that is the point. Arm a cross alert at the far
   edge: a 5m close through it voids the setup, and the order is cancelled.
7. Target: the price and its R. A wall under 1.5R from the entry on the
   trade side means no trade, whichever kind of wall. A vol strike that is
   not a wall, or an HTF level, under 1.5R is not a block in the break
   regime, where levels are giving way; in the sweep regime it is the
   target, and under 1.5R there is no trade.
8. Time: the displacement closed between 09:30 and 15:30 ET. The retrace may
   fill up to 30 minutes after the window. A displacement between 12:00 and
   13:30 needs the volume to show it (on 2026-10-16 the 12:00 and 13:00
   displacements carried 708 and 466 against 50 to 300 around them).

## Invalidation

- The order rests while the regime holds: there is no bar count. It is
  cancelled when a 5m bar closes through the far edge of the gap, when two
  GEX reads flip the regime, when price reaches the target unfilled (the
  trade never existed; do not chase into the wall), or at 15:30.
- Filled, then a 5m close beyond the structural extreme: close the position
  if the stop has not already done it.

## What this model is not

- A level touched, or given up by drift, without displacement.
- A displacement against the side. Watch it; it is not a trade.
- A second order in the same gap after a stop.
- An entry chased after price has left the gap: if the retrace never came,
  the trade never existed.
- A short placed a few points above the OI put wall, or a long under the OI
  call wall. On 2026-10-16 the baseline's losing short was placed 4 points
  above the 7700 put wall after a 70-point move; the winning one was placed 17
  points above it with the wall as the target.
