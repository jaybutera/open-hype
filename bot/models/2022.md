# Model: sweep, shift, retrace

The only setup you may trade. Three events in order at a significant level,
then an entry on the retrace. Every term below has one meaning; use it.

## Definitions

- **Significant level**: a price from the level list in the journal's step 1.
  Nothing else qualifies, however clean it looks.
- **Sweep**: a bar on the entry timeframe whose wick trades through the level
  and whose close is back on the original side of it. The sweep bar's extreme
  is the sweep wick. A close beyond the level is a break, not a sweep, and the
  model does not apply until price comes back and sweeps again.
- **Displacement**: the move away from the sweep, made of one or more bars
  with bodies at least twice the average body of the preceding 20 bars on the
  entry timeframe, leaving a gap between the wicks of the bar before and the
  bar after at least one of them.
- **Market structure shift (MSS)**: the displacement closes through the most
  recent swing on the entry timeframe that formed on the way into the level.
  For a sweep of a low, that is the last swing high before the sweep; for a
  sweep of a high, the last swing low. A wick through the swing is not a
  shift.
- **Fair value gap (FVG)**: the gap left by the displacement, between the
  wick of the bar before the displacement bar and the wick of the bar after it.
- **Order block (OB)**: the last bar of the opposite colour before the
  displacement began. Its open and close are the zone.
- **Opposing liquidity**: the nearest level from the level list on the far
  side of the entry, in the direction of the trade.

## Checklist

Fill every line in the journal before an order. A blank line means no trade.

1. Level swept, with its price and its name from the level list.
2. Sweep bar: ET time, the sweep wick price, the close.
3. Displacement bars: ET times. Average body of the preceding 20 bars and the
   displacement body that beat twice that.
4. MSS: the swing price that was closed through, and the ET time of the close.
5. Retrace zone: the FVG (both edges) or the OB (open and close). Prefer the
   FVG when the displacement left one that has not been traded back into;
   otherwise the OB. Write which and why.
6. Entry: a limit at the far half of the zone (the half nearer the sweep
   wick), or a market entry on the first entry-timeframe close back inside
   the zone that holds the zone. Write which.
7. Stop: a few ticks beyond the sweep wick. Never inside the zone.
8. Target: the opposing liquidity. Write its price and the R multiple it
   gives from the entry. Below 2R there is no trade.
9. Time: the sweep happened after 09:30 ET and before 11:30 ET, or after
   13:30 ET and before 15:30 ET. Outside those windows there is no trade.
10. Bias: the trade direction agrees with the 4h bias from step 1, or the
    bias is "none" and the swept level is a 4h level.

## Invalidation

- Price does not retrace into the zone within 12 entry-timeframe bars of the
  MSS: the setup is gone. Cancel the resting order.
- Price closes through the far edge of the zone before the order fills: the
  setup is gone. Cancel the resting order.
- Filled, then an entry-timeframe close beyond the sweep wick: the stop has
  done its job; if for any reason the position is still open, close it.

## What this model is not

- A level touched without a sweep. Watch it; it is not a trade.
- A sweep without displacement. Price may be about to break the level.
- An MSS on 1m against a 5m trend with no 5m sweep. The entry timeframe is 5m;
  1m only refines the entry inside a 5m setup.
- A second attempt at the same level after the first retrace failed, unless a
  new sweep and a new MSS form.
