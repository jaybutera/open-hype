# Model: sweep, shift, retrace (v3)

v2 with three definitions replaced after a mechanical scan of 95 SP500 days
(April to September 2026, 5m, 09:30 to 11:30 ET sweeps): the shift is a close
beyond the sweep bar, a failed break counts as a sweep, and the entry sits at
the near edge of the zone. `scripts/bot-scan.py` is the scan.

The only setup you may trade. Three events in order at a significant level,
then an entry on the retrace. Every term below has one meaning; use it.

## Definitions

- **Significant level**: a price from the level list in the journal's step 1.
  Nothing else qualifies, however clean it looks.
- **Sweep**: a bar on the entry timeframe whose wick trades through the level
  and whose close is back on the original side of it. The sweep bar's extreme
  is the sweep wick. A failed break is also a sweep: when one, two or three
  bars close beyond the level and the next bar closes back on the original
  side, that closing bar is the sweep bar and the extreme of the whole
  excursion is the sweep wick. Four or more closes beyond the level is a
  break, and the model does not apply until price comes back and sweeps
  again.
- **Displacement**: one of the three bars after the sweep bar, closing away
  from the level, with a body at least twice the average body of the 20 bars
  before the sweep bar, leaving a gap between the wick of the bar before it
  and the wick of the bar after it. No gap, no displacement.
- **Market structure shift (MSS)**: the displacement bar closes beyond the
  sweep bar's own extreme on the far side: below the sweep bar's low after a
  sweep of a high, above the sweep bar's high after a sweep of a low. Nothing
  else needs to be broken; do not go looking for swings further back.
- **Fair value gap (FVG)**: the gap left by the displacement, between the
  wick of the bar before the displacement bar and the wick of the bar after it.
  Its near edge is the edge price reaches first on the retrace: the lower
  edge after a short displacement, the upper edge after a long one.
- **Opposing liquidity**: the nearest level from the level list on the far
  side of the entry, in the direction of the trade.

## Checklist

Fill every line in the journal before an order. A blank line means no trade.

1. Level swept, with its price and its name from the level list.
2. Sweep bar: ET time, the sweep wick price, the close. For a failed break,
   the closes beyond the level too.
3. Displacement bar: ET time, its body, the average body of the 20 bars before
   the sweep bar, and the gap (the two wick prices).
4. MSS: the sweep bar's low or high that the displacement closed through.
5. Retrace zone: the FVG, both edges, once the bar after the displacement has
   closed. If the displacement left no gap, there is no trade.
6. Entry: a limit at the near edge of the zone. Place it when the bar after
   the displacement has closed, not before; the edge is not known until then.
7. Stop: a few ticks beyond the sweep wick. Never inside the zone.
8. Target: the opposing liquidity. Write its price and the R multiple it
   gives from the entry. Below 2R there is no trade; when the next level is
   further than 2R, 2R is still a fine target.
9. Time: the sweep happened after 09:30 ET and before 11:30 ET, or after
   13:30 ET and before 15:30 ET. Outside those windows there is no trade.
10. Bias: the trade direction agrees with the 4h direction from step 1.

## Invalidation

- Price does not retrace into the zone within 12 entry-timeframe bars of the
  displacement: the setup is gone. Cancel the resting order.
- Price closes through the far edge of the zone before the order fills: the
  setup is gone. Cancel the resting order.
- Filled, then an entry-timeframe close beyond the sweep wick: the stop has
  done its job; if for any reason the position is still open, close it.

## What this model is not

- A level touched without a sweep. Watch it; it is not a trade.
- A sweep without displacement. Price may be about to break the level.
- A displacement without a gap. A big bar that overlaps its neighbours is a
  push, not a displacement.
- An MSS on 1m against a 5m trend with no 5m sweep. The entry timeframe is 5m;
  1m is for reading the bar at the level, never for inventing a setup.
- A second attempt at the same level after the first retrace failed, unless a
  new sweep and a new displacement form.
