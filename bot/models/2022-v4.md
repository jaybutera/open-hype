# Model: displacement at a level, entry on the retrace (v4)

The 2022 model with its thresholds removed and the entry read on 1m. The v3
definitions (a body twice the 20-bar average, a three-bar window, a 5m gap)
were scanned over 132 sessions and showed no edge at any setting, and in the
sim they rejected all eleven sweeps of 2026-10-16 while the market fell 125
points. What stays is the shape: price reaches a level, displaces away from it
in the daily direction, leaves a gap, and the gap is the entry.

The only setup you may trade.

## Definitions

- **Significant level**: a price from the level list in the journal's step 1.
  Nothing else qualifies.
- **Displacement**: one 5m bar, or two in a row, moving away from the level in
  the trade direction with bodies that dwarf the bars around them, closing
  beyond the bar that reached the level, and leaving a gap. If you have to
  measure whether a bar is a displacement, it is not one. A level given up by
  a drift of small overlapping bars is a break without displacement; the
  retest of it from the other side is where the displacement may come.
- **Two forms**, by where price came from:
  - *Sweep*: price came from the trade side, wicked through the level, and the
    displacement took it back. The sweep wick is the structural extreme.
  - *Break*: price came from the other side and the displacement closed
    through the level. The origin of the displacement leg, the last 1m swing
    before it started, is the structural extreme.
  In negative gamma below zero gamma the session produces breaks; in positive
  gamma above zero gamma it produces sweeps. Both are the model.
- **Fair value gap (FVG)**: the gap the displacement left: on 5m, between the
  wicks of the bars either side of the displacement bar; when the 5m bars
  leave none, the 1m gap inside the displacement leg. The near edge is the
  edge price reaches first on the retrace.
- **Stop**: beyond the structural extreme. Never inside the gap. A price with
  a reason, not a round number of points.
- **Target**: 2R, unless a major gamma strike, zero gamma, or a daily or 4h
  level sits between 1.5R and 3R in the trade direction; then that price is
  the target (the strike price is touching, not the next one).

## Checklist

Fill every line in the journal before an order. A blank line means no trade.

1. Level: its price and its name from the level list.
2. Direction: the daily's, and the GEX regime read that goes with it.
3. Displacement: the bar or bars by ET time, their bodies against the bars
   around them, and the extreme they closed through.
4. Gap: both edges and which timeframe shows it.
5. Structural extreme, and the stop price beyond it.
6. Entry: a resting limit inside the gap, a quarter of the way in from the
   near edge, with the stop and target attached, placed once the bar after
   the displacement has closed. The paper engine fills a limit when a 1m bar
   closes through it, so a touch does not fill; that is the point. Arm a
   cross alert at the far edge: a 5m close through it voids the setup, and
   the order is cancelled.
7. Target: the price and its R. If the first major strike, zero gamma, or
   HTF level in the way is under 1.5R from the entry, there is no trade.
8. Time: the displacement closed between 09:30 and 12:00 ET or between 13:30
   and 15:30 ET. The retrace may fill up to 30 minutes after the window.

## Invalidation

- No retrace into the gap within 12 5m bars of the displacement: cancel.
- A 5m close through the far edge of the gap before the fill: cancel.
- Filled, then a 5m close beyond the structural extreme: close the position
  if the stop has not already done it.

## What this model is not

- A level touched, or given up by drift, without displacement.
- A displacement against the daily direction. Watch it; it is not a trade.
- A second order in the same gap after a stop.
- An entry chased after price has left the gap: if the retrace never came,
  the trade never existed.
- A short placed a few points above the put wall, or a long under the call
  wall. On 2026-10-16 the baseline's losing short was placed 4 points above
  the 7700 put wall after a 70-point move; the winning one was placed 17
  points above it with the wall as the target.
